\begin{abstract}
  The team containerised a company's services as Docker stacks on a
  Swarm of three Azure virtual machines that share an NFS disk. One node
  is the only public entry: nginx ends HTTPS with a Let's Encrypt
  certificate and routes each subdomain to its stack. The basic, app and
  atenea stacks each have a local Compose file and a production file,
  and the team builds their images and keeps them in its own registry.
  Stateless services run two replicas and update one at a time with
  automatic rollback, and both databases have a verified daily backup.
  The main result is that data outlives containers: the app's database
  moved between nodes with its data intact, and a restore drill brought
  a deleted Moodle course back in 21~seconds. The main gap is that the
  backups still share a disk with the data they protect.
\end{abstract}
